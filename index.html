01 At a glance

Clicks and engagement rose even where reach slipped.

Maxen Power: the headline

  • LinkedIn clicks rose 48% and engagement rate reached 17.7% on 13 posts; static posts did the work.
  • Facebook views more than doubled to 640 and viewers nearly tripled to 268.
  • YouTube views edged up to 487, now spread across several Shorts, but watch time fell to 1.6 hrs.
  • Trustpilot removed 8 reviews and kept 3 online; negative reviews more than doubled to 28.

MPS: the headline

  • LinkedIn clicks rose 31% to 11.9K and new followers rose to 194, on four fewer posts.
  • Instagram views fell to 72K after August's record, with fewer reels and stories shared.
  • Instagram followers still grew to 750 and non-follower reach edged up to 34%.
